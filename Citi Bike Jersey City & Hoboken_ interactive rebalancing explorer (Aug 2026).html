Source: Citi Bike trip data, Jersey City and Hoboken, August 2026; hourly weather from Open-Meteo. Read with care: no dock inventory exists in trip files, so fill and drain are estimated from net flows; arrivals from Manhattan are missing, which inflates drain and deflates fill. Risk scores rise with station volume, so zone differences show where the workload sits, not unequal treatment. One month cannot show seasonality.
